FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about emergency and after hours dental care in Santa Rosa.

Do you offer after hours dentist appointments in Santa Rosa?

Yes. We are open 24 hours, so you can see an after hours dentist at night, on weekends and on holidays. Call 707-230-6722 and we'll arrange the fastest appointment available.

What counts as a dental emergency?

Severe toothache, swelling or abscess, broken or knocked-out teeth, uncontrolled bleeding, and injuries to the mouth or jaw are all emergencies. If you're unsure, call us and we'll guide you.

Can I get a same day dentist appointment?

In most cases, yes. We prioritize urgent cases so you can be seen quickly, often the same day you call.

What should I do while waiting to be seen?

Rinse gently with warm water, apply a cold compress to reduce swelling, and avoid placing aspirin directly on the gum. For a knocked-out tooth, keep it moist (in milk or saliva) and come in right away.

Is your emergency dental care affordable?

We aim to keep urgent care accessible and transparent. Call us to discuss your situation and what to expect before your visit.
